# Pilot MoU / approval — template

> A starting text for the ward/ULB's legal team. Not legal advice; replace every `‹…›`. The pilot must not start without the signed document (Phase 6 §3).

**Between** ‹Ward / ULB / Department name› ("the Authority") **and** ‹Operator name› ("the Operator") **for** the Namma Seva public-works transparency pilot in ‹Ward number & name› ("the Pilot").

1. **Purpose.** To record selected public infrastructure projects, milestones, proofs and payments on the MST blockchain so residents can verify them, and to give residents a channel to raise grievances.
2. **Scope & duration.** ‹N› real projects (‹list›), ‹1 official, 2–3 auditors, 2 contractors› from ‹date› to ‹date›, on MST ‹testnet / mainnet›. Payments are recorded in **ledger mode**: money continues to move through the Authority's existing treasury (PFMS/e-payment); the chain records approvals and payment references.
3. **Roles.** The Authority nominates officials and auditors and approves contractors; wallets are issued and held by the individuals themselves. The Operator runs the platform and does not hold approval powers. Administrative control of the contracts rests with a 3-of-5 multisig with a 48-hour timelock whose signers are: ‹list›.
4. **Data.** Project, budget, milestone and payment-reference data shared by the Authority is **published publicly and permanently** (RTI Act s.4 proactive disclosure). No personal data of officials or contractors beyond their public role and wallet address is recorded on-chain. Citizen data is handled under the Privacy Notice; the **Data Fiduciary** for citizen data is ‹Authority / Operator› and the **Grievance Officer** is ‹name, contact›.
5. **Security & incidents.** The Operator maintains the controls in the repository's `docs/security` and `docs/runbooks`, reports incidents to CERT-In within 6 hours and informs the Authority's nodal officer immediately.
6. **Service levels (pilot).** Best-effort availability with monitoring and an on-call contact; target recovery of the read model ≤ 4 h; no obligation of 24×7 support.
7. **Grievances.** Citizens' grievances that reach the escalation threshold are shown to the responsible official, who is expected to respond within ‹7› days; responses are public.
8. **Records & audit.** The Authority and its auditors may inspect the Operator's logs relating to the Pilot; logs are retained 180 days.
9. **Intellectual property.** Software is released under ‹MIT›; data belongs to the Authority and is published as open data.
10. **Limitations.** The platform records and displays information; it does not replace statutory approvals or payment processes. Automated flags are prompts for review, not findings.
11. **Exit.** Either party may end the Pilot on ‹30› days' notice. On exit the public on-chain record remains; the Operator deletes off-chain personal data on request and hands over exports.
12. **Governing law.** Laws of India; courts at ‹Bengaluru›.

Signed: ‹Authority signatory› · ‹Operator signatory› · Date ‹…› · Witnesses ‹…›
